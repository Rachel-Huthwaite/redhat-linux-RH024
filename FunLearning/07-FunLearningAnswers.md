# 07 - Fun Learning Answers: Editing Files with Vim

Use these answers to check your responses from `07-FunLearningQuestions.md`.

---

### Answers

1. **Default Mode:**
   * **Normal mode**[cite: 7].

2. **Switching to Insert Mode:**
   * Press `i`[cite: 7]. You will see `-- INSERT --` displayed in the bottom-left corner of the terminal window[cite: 7].

3. **Returning to Normal Mode:**
   * Press the `Esc` (Escape) key[cite: 7].

4. **Copy & Paste Shortcuts:**
   * Copy (Yank): `yy`[cite: 7]
   * Paste (Put): `p`[cite: 7]

5. **Multiple Pastes:**
   * `10p`[cite: 7].

6. **Undo & Redo:**
   * Undo: `u`[cite: 7]
   * Redo: `Ctrl + r`[cite: 7]

7. **Force Quitting:**
   * `:q!`[cite: 7].

8. **Interactive Practice:**
   * `vimtutor`[cite: 7].
