# 01 - Fun Learning Quiz: Linux Distributions

Test your active recall on the concepts introduced in Lesson 01. Try answering these without referring to your notes!

---

### Questions

1. **Ecosystem Pipeline:** What are the four main tiers of the Red Hat open-source ecosystem pipeline, starting from raw community projects to enterprise production?
2. **Distro Core:** What single software component is shared across all Linux distributions regardless of vendor?
3. **Distribution Differences:** Name three main characteristics that distinguish one Linux distribution from another.
4. **Package Management:** What package management format is used by RHEL, Fedora, and CentOS Stream, and why is it incompatible with `apt` out of the box?
5. **Role of CentOS Stream:** What specific function does CentOS Stream serve in relation to future RHEL releases?
6. **Filesystem Standards:** What does FHS stand for, and why does Red Hat strictly adhere to it?
7. **RHEL Lifecycle:** Describe the release schedule and two main support phases for a major version of Red Hat Enterprise Linux.
8. **Upstream Contributions:** Name two major open-source infrastructure projects created or heavily contributed to by Red Hat.
9. **Developer Subscription:** What key benefits are included in the no-cost Red Hat Developer Subscription for individuals?
10. **System Upgrades:** What is the name of the tool used to perform major version in-place upgrades on RHEL?
