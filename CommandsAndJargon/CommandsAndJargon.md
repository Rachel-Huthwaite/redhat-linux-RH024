#  Commands & Jargon

This package is dedicated to keeping track of every command learnt and every
new piece of jargon picked up along the way — a running glossary built to
grow my technical vocabulary as I move through the course.

- [`Commands.md`](./Commands.md) — every command learnt, what it does, and
  which lesson it came from
- [`Jargon.md`](./Jargon.md) — every new term encountered, defined in my own
  words

Both files are updated after every lesson, alongside the matching
[Lesson Breakdown](../LessonBreakdowns).
